# KonsultanK3.com Repository & Relaunch Blueprint

This project repository contains the architectural blueprints, technical SEO recovery specifications, backlink audit, and content strategies for relaunching **`KonsultanK3.com`** as an independent, authoritative K3 (occupational safety and health) information and consulting portal.

---

## Strategic Overview
* **Domain:** `konsultank3.com`
* **Historical Age:** 15+ continuous years (Wayback Machine records active from 2011 to 2026).
* **Backlink Heritage:** Authentic educational citations from major Indonesian universities (`ub.ac.id`, `poltekkes-smg.ac.id`, `maranatha.edu`, `unjaya.ac.id`, `widyatama.ac.id`).
* **Clean History:** Never penalized or hijacked for gambling (*judi slot*), adult content, or foreign spam.
* **Objective:** Capture high-intent organic search queries for K3 training and consulting, turning them into direct training and consulting inquiries through the site's own contact and WhatsApp channels.
